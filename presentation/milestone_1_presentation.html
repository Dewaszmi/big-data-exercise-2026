Business Value & Problem Statement

The Industry Problem: Flight delays cost the aviation industry over $30 Billion annually in wasted fuel, crew overtime, and passenger re-accommodations.


Strategic Objectives of This Pipeline:

  • Operational Decision Support: Equip airport operators and airlines with quantified delay-cause telemetry (Carrier vs. Weather vs. Air Traffic).
  • Passenger & Travel Tech Value: Serve historical risk indicators to forecast schedule vulnerabilities before booking.
  • Architectural Optimization: Eliminate raw file scan overhead by establishing partition pruning and columnar physical layouts.

Engine Architecture: Why DuckDB?

Rather than provisioning a heavy database server (PostgreSQL/Spark), we utilize DuckDB as an embedded in-process OLAP engine:

  • Zero Network Overhead: Direct execution inside Python process memory space.
  • Vectorized SIMD Execution: Columnar vector batches process millions of values per CPU cycle.
  • Direct Disk Querying (Streaming): Evaluates files without loading the entire raw dataset into RAM (Zero-Copy).
  • Benchmark Performance: Full 400,000-row multi-column aggregations complete in ~120 milliseconds on local hardware.

Business & Technical Anomalies

  • 1. Fractional Incident Counts (82.3% of rows):
    Discovery: Columns such as carrier_ct = 0.5 or 1.45 are floats.
    Domain Explanation: BTS regulations mandate proportional attribution when multiple causes contribute to a single flight delay.
  • 2. Component Sum Discrepancy (9 instances):
    Discovery: Sum of individual delay causes does not equal arr_delay.
    Technical Source: Retroactive carrier filing adjustments and legacy manual adjustments.
  • 3. 100% Cancellation Episodes (270 instances):
    Discovery: Months where arr_cancelled == arr_flights.
    Real-world Source: Severe atmospheric phenomena (hurricanes/blizzards) and the April 2020 pandemic lockdown.

Data Limitations: Blind Spots

Understanding what raw telemetry does NOT capture is critical for realistic engineering:

  • Aggregation Granularity: Records are monthly summaries. Individual flight paths, tail numbers, and minute-by-minute gate telemetry are aggregated away.
  • Micro-Weather Telemetry: Records state "Weather Delay", but do not record wind speed, icing, or runway visibility indices.
  • Passenger Consequences: No visibility into missed passenger connections, baggage delays, or economic compensation claims.
  • Airspace Congestion Dynamics: Does not reveal en-route holding patterns vs. ground stop orders.

Risks, Ethics & Algorithmic Fairness

  • Carrier Bias Risk: Regional airlines operating predominantly in high-latitude or mountainous terrains naturally accumulate higher weather delays. Ranking airlines without weather normalization leads to unfair commercial penalization.
  • Privacy & PII: The dataset strictly contains institutional operational records. Zero personally identifiable information (PII) regarding crew or travelers exists.
  • Downstream Model Vulnerability: Feeding uncleaned outliers (e.g., COVID shutdown months) into forecasting models causes severe parameter drift.
